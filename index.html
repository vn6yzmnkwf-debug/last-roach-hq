How to buy LROACH on mobile

The creator completed a buy and a sell using a mobile wallet on November 20, 2026. Desktop QR / WalletConnect failed in testing. The mobile wallet route below worked for that wallet, but it is not a guarantee for other wallets or future trades.

  1. Open MetaMask on your phone. Use its in-app browser, not a separate desktop QR connection. If you do not have a wallet yet, set one up securely and keep your recovery phrase private.
  2. Have a small amount of ETH on the Base network. Confirm your wallet shows Base (chain ID 8453) and leave enough ETH for network fees. ETH on a different network is not automatically available on Base.
  3. Open the official LROACH page on Clanker from the MetaMask in-app browser, using the link below. If this page is open in Safari or Chrome, copy the Clanker URL and paste it into the wallet browser.
  4. Check the quote before you approve. Verify the exact token address, ETH amount, estimated LROACH, 3% launch-pool buy tax where applicable, wallet fees, slippage and gas. If your chosen platform supports it, also check an indicative LROACH-to-ETH sell quote for the same contract. A buy estimate does not prove the token can be sold. Do not bypass unknown price impact, high-fee or sell-restriction warnings. Only you decide whether to confirm.
  5. Verify the result. Check the wallet activity and, if needed, open the transaction in the Base block explorer. A quote alone is not a completed purchase.

Token details

Last Roach is a speculative meme token on Base. Eligible buys and sells in the official Clanker v5 Uniswap v4 launch pool carry a 3% fee. Other routes may not carry that fee. Of eligible trade volume, 2.5% goes to the creator before gas and taxes and 0.5% goes to the protocol. Crypto assets can lose their entire value. There are no promised returns or holder rewards.

A read-only simulation at Base block 57,867,972 demonstrated a buy and subsequent sell. It did not execute real trades and does not guarantee future liquidity, prices or execution. Simulation reference block.
